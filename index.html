Questions I'm Asking

I'm constantly exploring systems where people, incentives, and ideas intersect. Here are some of the questions that guide my curiosity.

  • How are communities and networks built and sustained?
  • What drives the psychology of decision-making under uncertainty?
  • How do people choose to lead, and why do others follow?
  • How do cities foster innovation and creativity?
  • How will financial infrastructure evolve in the next decade?
  • What role will agentic commerce, stablecoins, and programmable money play in the economy?
  • How can startups serve as vehicles for innovation?
  • What are the underlying incentives and economics behind sports?
  • How do markets function as coordination mechanisms?
  • Why do power laws and asymmetric outcomes dominate systems?
